# REELCLICKS.IN — Website Guide

A simple guide for the REELCLICKS team: how to update the website yourself.
No coding needed. Everything is done in the **admin panel**.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open **reelclicks.in/studio** on your phone or computer.
2. Click **Log in** and use the Google account (or email) that was invited to the Sanity project.
3. You'll see a menu on the left. That's your admin panel.

> First time? Ask your developer to invite your email at sanity.io/manage → Members.

**The golden rule:** after any change, press the green **Publish** button (bottom right).
Nothing changes on the website until you publish. Published changes appear on the site within a few seconds.

---

## 2. What's in the menu

| Menu item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Enquiries (Leads)** | Every message sent through the website's contact form |
| **Portfolio Videos** | Your films and reels (Portfolio page, Home page, city pages) |
| **Portfolio Categories** | Weddings, Brand & Promotional, Corporate, Events, Travel & Lifestyle |
| **Services** | The 8 services, their descriptions and packages |
| **Team** | Huzef, Adil and any crew members (photos, bios, phone numbers) |
| **Testimonials** | Client reviews shown on the Home page |
| **Numbers (Counters)** | "Weddings Filmed", "Brands Served" etc. on the Home page |
| **FAQs** | Questions and answers on the Services page |
| **City Pages** | Nashik, Malegaon, Manmad, Nandgaon pages for Google |
| **Pages (Google & texts)** | The Google title/description of each page, plus some page texts |
| **Site Settings** | Logo, contact numbers, email, Instagram, showreel, business hours |

**Changing the order:** in Portfolio, Services, Team, Testimonials, Numbers and FAQs you can
**drag items up and down** to change the order they appear on the website.

---

## 3. Seeing and managing enquiries

1. Click **Enquiries (Leads)** → **🟡 New**. New enquiries from the website appear here.
2. Click one to see the name, phone, service, date, location, budget and message.
3. After you've called or messaged them, change the **Status**:
   - 🔵 **Contacted**: you've replied
   - 🟢 **Booked**: date confirmed
   - ⚪ **Closed**: not going ahead
4. Use **Your notes** for anything private (price quoted, follow-up date…). Clients never see this.
5. Press **Publish** to save.

You'll also receive each enquiry by **email** (once email is set up), with a "Reply on WhatsApp" button.

## 4. Adding a portfolio video

### Step A: upload the video to Bunny Stream
Videos are **not** uploaded into the admin panel. Big video files go to **Bunny Stream** so they play fast on every phone.

1. Log in at **bunny.net** → **Stream** → your video library → **Upload**.
2. When it finishes processing, click the video and copy its **Video ID**
   (a long code like `a1b2c3d4-…`).

### Step B: add it to the website
1. Admin panel → **Portfolio Videos** → **+** (create new).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: e.g. "Vineyard Vows"
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**
   - **Category**: Weddings, Brand & Promotional, etc.
   - **Cover image**: a beautiful still from the film (landscape for films, portrait for reels)
   - **Full video** → *Where is the video hosted?* → **Bunny Stream** → paste the **Bunny video ID**
   - **Hover preview** (optional): a short 5–10 second clip, also from Bunny
   - **Video shape**: *Wide film (16:9)* or *Vertical reel (9:16)*
   - **Show on Home page**: switch on for your 5–6 best films
   - **Location**, **Shoot date**, **Client / couple name**, **Short story**
3. Optional **Story & Case Study** tab: a longer write-up, behind-the-scenes photos, and a testimonial.
4. Press **Publish**.

The film now appears on the Portfolio page (in its category), on its own story page, and on the
Home page if you switched that on.

## 5. Changing images

- **Your logo:** Site Settings → Brand → **Logo** (SVG or transparent PNG).
- **Leader photos:** Team → Huzef / Adil → **Portrait** (portrait shape, at least 1200 px tall).
- **Service card images:** Services → choose a service → **Card image**.
- **About page photos:** Pages → About → **Hero image** and **Behind-the-scenes photos**.

**Tips for great images:**
- Use large, sharp photos (at least 2000 px wide for wide images). The website makes them small and fast automatically.
- After uploading, click the image and drag the **circle (hotspot)** onto the most important part,
  such as the couple's faces. The website will always keep that part visible when cropping.
- Fill in the **Short description**. It helps blind visitors and Google.
- Never use photos of celebrities or people who haven't agreed to it.

---

## 6. The showreel (Home page video)

1. Upload your 60–90 second showreel to Bunny Stream, and a **vertical 9:16 cut** for phones.
2. Site Settings → **Showreel** tab:
   - **Showreel (desktop, 16:9)** → Bunny → paste ID
   - **Showreel (mobile, 9:16 cut)** → Bunny → paste ID
   - **Showreel cover image**: shown instantly while the video loads
3. **Publish.**

## 7. Other common updates

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Change a phone number or email | Site Settings → Contact |
| Update business hours | Site Settings → Contact → Business hours |
| Add a testimonial | Testimonials → **+** (quote, name, event type, city) |
| Update the counters | Numbers (Counters) → click a number → change it |
| Show package prices | Services → a service → **Packages** tab → switch on *Show packages* → add prices (leave empty for "Get Quote") |
| Add/edit an FAQ | FAQs |
| Add a crew member | Team → **+** (leave *Leadership* switched off) |
| Change what Google shows for a page | Pages → choose the page → *Google & Social Sharing* |
| Edit the Home page manifesto text | Pages → Home → *Manifesto* |
| Edit the About story, vision, mission, values | Pages → About |
| Add a new city page | City Pages → **+** (write unique text for each city) |

---

## 8. Good habits

- **Publish** after every change, then refresh the website to check it.
- Keep **testimonials and numbers real**. Visitors (and Google) trust honest content.
- Reply to **New** enquiries quickly; the website promises "We reply within 2 hours".
- Mistakes are easy to undo: open a document → the **clock icon** (top right) → pick an earlier version → *Restore*.
